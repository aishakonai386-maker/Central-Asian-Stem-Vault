# Central Asian STEM Vault

Central Asian STEM Vault is a trilingual online platform that provides free STEM study guides, research tools, and scholarship information for students in Central Asia.

## Impact

- Available in English, Russian, and Kazakh
- Designed to improve access to STEM learning and scholarship opportunities for students in rural Central Asia
- Provides free study guides, research tools, and scholarship resources
- Reached 2,000+ users/visits

## My Role

- Developing and continuously upgrading based on users' needs. Trying to make long-lasting changes in education making it more accessible and easy to understand wfor confused students while creating equity.

## Features

- STEM study guides
- Research tools
- Scholarship information
- English, Russian, and Kazakh language support
- Resources
- Career Fields For STEM Students
- Strategy for Middle to High schoolers

## Tech Stack

- JAVA
- HTML

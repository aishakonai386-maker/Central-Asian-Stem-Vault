STEM FIELDS

Explore STEM Fields

STEM is a wide world of possibilities. Whether you live in a big city or a small village, your interests, curiosity, and effort can lead you into any of these fields.

What does STEM stand for?

STEM is an acronym for four connected fields. They often overlap — a scientist uses technology, an engineer uses math, and a programmer uses science. You do not have to choose one forever — many careers combine several of them.

S

Science

Understanding the natural world — biology, chemistry, physics, Earth science.

T

Technology

Tools, software, and systems that solve real problems.

E

Engineering

Designing and building things — from bridges to medical devices.

M

Mathematics

The language behind science, engineering, and technology.

Majors you can study

These are some of the most common STEM majors at universities around the world. Many are also available at universities in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, and Tajikistan.

COMPUTER SCIENCE

Computer Science & IT

Study how computers work, how to build software, and how to solve problems with code. One of the most flexible STEM fields — you can work from anywhere in the world.

Majors include:
  • Computer Science
  • Software Engineering
  • Data Science
  • Cybersecurity
ENGINEERING

Engineering

Apply science and math to design real things — machines, structures, electronics, energy systems. Engineering is very hands-on and in high demand in Central Asia.

Majors include:
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Chemical Engineering
LIFE SCIENCES

Biology & Medicine

Study living things — from cells to ecosystems. This path can lead to medicine, biotechnology, genetics, or environmental science.

Majors include:
  • Biology
  • Biotechnology
  • Neuroscience
  • Public Health
PHYSICAL SCIENCES

Physics & Chemistry

Explore the fundamental rules of the universe — energy, matter, and how they interact. Physicists and chemists work in research, industry, and education.

Majors include:
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Materials Science
  • Astrophysics
MATHEMATICS

Mathematics & Statistics

Go beyond formulas — math is the foundation of science, economics, AI, and engineering. Mathematicians are highly valued in every field.

Majors include:
  • Pure Mathematics
  • Applied Mathematics
  • Statistics
  • Actuarial Science
ENVIRONMENT

Environmental & Earth Sciences

Study climate, water, land, and how humans interact with the planet. Especially important for Central Asia — water, agriculture, and desertification are huge local issues.

Majors include:
  • Environmental Science
  • Geology
  • Climate Science
  • Hydrology

Where do STEM careers lead?

A STEM degree does not lock you into one job. It opens doors in many directions — in your country, abroad, or online.

TECHNOLOGY

Software Engineer

Build apps, websites, and systems used by millions.

HEALTHCARE

Doctor or Researcher

Diagnose, treat, or study diseases to improve lives.

ENERGY

Renewable Energy Engineer

Design solar, wind, and hydro systems for the future.

DATA

Data Scientist

Find patterns in data to solve business and social problems.

EDUCATION

Teacher or Professor

Inspire the next generation of scientists and thinkers.

RESEARCH

Scientist

Discover new knowledge in universities or research institutes.

Where you live does not limit what you can study.

Many students from small towns in Central Asia go on to study at universities around the world — MIT, Oxford, Nazarbayev University, and beyond. The first step is always the same: pick a field that interests you, and start learning. Everything else follows.

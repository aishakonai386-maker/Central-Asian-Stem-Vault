About Central Asian STEM Vault

A student-built initiative working to make STEM opportunities accessible to high schoolers across Central Asia — especially those outside big cities.

Why this exists

Central Asian STEM Vault started as an Instagram page — a place to share STEM opportunities, competitions, and scholarships that too many students never hear about.

The idea came from a simple observation: students in small towns and rural areas are just as talented and curious as students in big cities — but they often have less access to information. Programs, olympiads, scholarships, research opportunities — all of it exists, but the knowledge is scattered and hard to find.

So we brought it together. One place. In three languages. Accessible from a phone. Free for everyone.

What we do

Gather

We collect STEM opportunities, scholarships, competitions, and resources in one place, so students don't have to search across dozens of websites.

Explain

We explain how things work — how to do research, how to prepare for competitions, how to build a strategy — in clear, simple language.

Translate

Everything is available in English, Russian, and Kazakh — so students can learn in the language they're most comfortable with.

A message to students

If you're reading this from a small town, a village, or a place where no one around you is applying to international programs — you are exactly who this is for.

You don't need money, connections, or a famous school to start. You need curiosity, internet access, and a willingness to try. Everything else — we'll help you find it here.

Your starting point doesn't decide your ending point. Start today.
